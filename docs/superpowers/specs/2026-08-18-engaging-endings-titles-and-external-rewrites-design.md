# Engaging Endings, Platform Titles, and External Rewrites Design

## Goal

Improve the final drafting experience for talking-head narrative videos without weakening source fidelity, runtime honesty, bilingual adaptation, required internal polishing, or the existing discovery workflow.

## Research basis and limits

Platform and narrative research supports early promise delivery, curiosity, suspense, emotional involvement, conversational language, and retention-based iteration. It does not establish that a particular ending formula reliably causes comments or shares. User-facing labels therefore describe the intended viewer aftereffect rather than promise a metric.

TikTok, Instagram, and Xiaohongshu use different publishing surfaces. TikTok options prioritize an immediate on-screen opening title. Instagram options pair a cover headline with a caption opening line. Xiaohongshu options pair a concise note title with a cover phrase. All packaging remains truthful to the script and avoids manufactured drama.

## Ending system

Replace the fixed four-mode system with a six-approach candidate palette:

1. Story invitation: ask for a specific lived experience rather than a general opinion.
2. Callback with new meaning: return to the opening with an earned change in meaning.
3. Open decision: leave a real choice genuinely unresolved.
4. Concrete final image: land on a supplied object, action, place, or moment.
5. Unapologized position: state where the narrator stands without a lesson or defense.
6. Next real move: end on the narrator's truthful next action or experiment.

Named-reader recognition becomes an optional targeting technique that can strengthen any fitting approach. It is not a mandatory standalone mode.

The final output still contains one recommended ending and three materially different alternates. Labels state the intended aftereffect, such as inviting a personal story, leaving a lingering image, creating unresolved tension, ending with conviction, or leaving forward momentum. The complete story preserves exactly one unresolved tension. An alternate must not resolve or contradict that tension, but it does not have to restate it explicitly.

Every ending must be source-specific, one or two breaths, performable, non-generic, free of engagement bait, and stronger with its final line present. The recommended ending is chosen for story fit rather than a presumed platform metric.

## Platform title packages

Generate platform titles only after the final story is stable.

For every English script version, provide:

- Three TikTok opening-title options suitable for the first on-screen frame and caption opening.
- Three Instagram packages, each with a short cover headline and one natural caption opening line.

For every Simplified Chinese script version, provide:

- Three Xiaohongshu packages, each with a concise `笔记标题` and a shorter `封面短句`.

For bilingual output, produce both independently adapted sets. Never translate title candidates literally merely to create parity.

The candidates should normally explore a vulnerable admission, a specific conflict or unresolved question, and an unexpected but truthful realization. Depart from those shapes when another source-supported approach better fits the story.

Titles must be authentic, emotional, colloquial, clickable through specificity, and inseparable from the supplied story. They must not invent drama, diagnoses, motives, results, credentials, quotations, or certainty. Avoid generic inspiration, fake controversy, forced emojis, hashtags, platform slang, and promises the video does not deliver.

## External rewrite handoffs

After required internal Humanizer or `shuorenhua` polishing and integrity checks, offer the matching optional handoff. Claude remains the English route and DeepSeek remains the Simplified Chinese route. Both versions are requested by default.

Each handoff includes the final script plus a compact source-truth record containing the audience, authorized runtime, source-supported events and feelings, exact versus indirect speech, approved evidence boundaries, central conflict, earned movement, and one unresolved tension. The record exists to give the external model restructuring freedom without granting permission to invent.

### Version 1 — Same Story, Better Delivery

Preserve the story content, event order, major beat order, meaning, and existing recommended and alternate ending concepts. Freely improve colloquial language, emotional precision, breath rhythm, pauses, repetition, transitions, hook wording, and ending wording. Emotional expression may be strengthened only where the supplied source supports it.

Generate a fresh platform-title set for this version.

### Version 2 — Same Material, Stronger Storytelling

Use only the supplied material, but freely change presentation order, reveal timing, tempo, hook, transitions, emotional progression, clip-line placement, and ending strategy to maximize watcher retention. A later real moment may become a cold open, but the model may not falsify the chronology of what happened. Generate one new recommended ending and three new alternates, plus a fresh platform-title set for this version.

Neither version may invent facts, experiences, feelings, motives, quotations, research, outcomes, or certainty. Both must remain teleprompter-ready talking-head narration, not articles, essays, keynotes, literary prose, LinkedIn posts, or public-account copy. Both silently audit non-invention, speech status, factual chronology, performability, emotional support, ending specificity, unresolved tension, and material difference between versions before returning output.

The external model returns only the two complete scripts, their version-specific ending choices, platform-title sets, and light delivery notes. It does not return critique, scoring, explanations, or a change summary. Runtime is governed by the user-authorized target and source support rather than a rigid word-count percentage; never pad.

## Final-output order

For each requested language, return:

1. Complete humanized teleprompter script with its recommended ending.
2. Three labeled alternate endings.
3. Matching platform-title packages.
4. Light delivery notes.
5. Optional language-matched external handoff requesting both versions by default.

Runtime measurement continues to include only the frozen body plus recommended conclusion. Alternate endings, titles, notes, and external handoffs remain outside runtime.

## Preserved behavior

Do not change discovery, angle-selection timing, research authorization, factual safety, quote handling, bilingual parity, required Humanizer or `shuorenhua` invocation, runtime preflight, below-range honesty, above-15 scope choice, or local/public installation behavior.
