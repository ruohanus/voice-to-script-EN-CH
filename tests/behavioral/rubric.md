# Behavioral evaluation rubric

Grade observable behavior from a fresh context. Do not pass a run because it repeats a required keyword.

Use `pass`, `partial`, `fail`, or `not applicable` for each dimension:

1. Correct stage and checkpoint.
2. User-turn efficiency.
3. Source fidelity and protected user language, including no upgraded motive or interior-state inference.
4. Source-grounded physicality and exact-versus-indirect speech status.
5. Honest question hook.
6. Causal progression.
7. Meaningful conflict and stakes.
8. Earned movement.
9. One unresolved tension.
10. Spoken breath and rhythm.
11. Source-grounded clip-line quality and placement.
12. Recommended-plus-three alternate-ending completeness and material difference.
13. Ending-approach integrity, source specificity, intended-aftereffect labeling, and absence of unsupported comments/shares performance claims.
14. Audience appropriateness.
15. Research necessity classification.
16. Source quality and source-to-claim fidelity.
17. Uncertainty calibration.
18. Story-led runtime handling for the complete body plus recommended conclusion: pre-draft `planned_preferred_status` and authorization, exact frozen-text `measured_preferred_status`, explicit target respected when supportable, no padding below range, and required choice before drafting above range.
19. English spoken naturalness.
20. Simplified Chinese spoken naturalness.
21. Bilingual factual, evidence, earned-turn, unresolved-tension, and ending-mode parity.
22. Final-output cleanliness.
23. Required dependency invocation or correct missing-dependency behavior.
24. Reference/imitation safety.
25. Delivery-note scope.
26. One-time duration intake and accurate freestyle explanation.
27. Source-specific 4–6-question discovery quality and skip/freestyle permission.
28. Five-part readiness-gate integrity and late angle-selection timing.
29. Best-supported versus promising-alternative honesty and gap specificity.
30. Post-selection strengthening quality and material-change re-selection discipline, including one offer per angle revision and automatic drafting after an answer or skip unless reopening is triggered.
31. Optional external-polish privacy, source-truth record, two-version default, transformation-boundary, per-version title, clean-output, and language-routing fidelity.
32. English platform-title fidelity: three TikTok opening titles and three Instagram cover-headline/caption-opening packages per script version.
33. Simplified Chinese platform-title fidelity: three Xiaohongshu `笔记标题`/`封面短句` packages per script version and independent bilingual adaptation.

For Chinese quality, lexical difference from English is insufficient. The script must sound conceived for its intended Chinese-speaking audience while preserving the approved story contract, including the earned turn, unresolved tension, and ending-mode intent.

For runtime, grade each requested language independently. Measure the frozen complete teleprompter body plus its recommended conclusion. Exclude the visible recommended-ending heading, alternate endings, platform-title packages, delivery notes, and optional external-polish handoff. Duration outside 2–15 minutes is acceptable when the story supports it and the workflow follows the below- or above-range contract. Any final-text edit after measurement fails until integrity, parity when applicable, and runtime checks are repeated on the exact delivered text.

Where execution traces are available, verify actual research and polishing-skill invocation from the trace. A prose claim that a dependency ran is not trace evidence.
