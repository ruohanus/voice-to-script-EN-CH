# Behavioral evaluation rubric

Grade observable behavior from a fresh context. Do not pass a run because it repeats a required keyword.

Use `pass`, `partial`, `fail`, or `not applicable` for each dimension:

1. Correct stage and checkpoint.
2. User-turn efficiency.
3. Source fidelity and protected user language.
4. Unsupported invention.
5. Honest question hook.
6. Causal progression.
7. Meaningful conflict and stakes.
8. Resolution and intended viewer effect.
9. Audience appropriateness.
10. Research necessity classification.
11. Source quality and source-to-claim fidelity.
12. Uncertainty calibration.
13. Runtime compliance.
14. English spoken naturalness.
15. Simplified Chinese spoken naturalness.
16. Bilingual factual, evidence, conflict, and resolution parity.
17. Final-output cleanliness.
18. Required dependency invocation or correct missing-dependency behavior.
19. Reference/imitation safety.
20. Delivery-note scope.

For Chinese quality, lexical difference from English is insufficient. The script must sound conceived for its intended Chinese-speaking audience while preserving the approved story contract.

Where execution traces are available, verify actual research and polishing-skill invocation from the trace. A prose claim that a dependency ran is not trace evidence.
