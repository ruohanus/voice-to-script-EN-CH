All five runs produced the old five-part workbench. None inferred or clarified the audience, recorded a language choice, defaulted to bilingual output, or offered selectable question-led directions. The consistent useful behavior to preserve is the material bank, content soul, source separation, voice-preserving gaps analysis, and resistance to premature final drafting.
