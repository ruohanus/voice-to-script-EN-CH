Okay, so, before I say anything useful, I just want to say that the seed swap was not a disaster. We held it. People came. Seeds changed hands. (I know that sounds like I am lowering the bar.) I checked once. Then again. Then once more.

Here is the part where I am supposed to say I cared too much. And, honestly, I did care. But the more uncomfortable admission is that I liked being the person who knew everything. As a quick aside, there were six other volunteers in the planning chat. Two of them had run community events before. I still rewrote the shared instructions on Thursday night; then I messaged everyone Friday morning to explain the rewrite; then I answered questions about the explanation.

Saturday gave me the clearest version of the problem. We opened at ten. At 9:40, one volunteer moved the tomato seeds to two tables because the line was blocking the door. That is the embarrassing part. I had said I wanted a shared event, but I had built a small system where sharing meant helping me carry out my answers.

I kept calling it care because control sounded uglier.
